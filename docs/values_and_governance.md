# VALUES AND GOVERNANCE
1. Material Reality & Financial Risk
We recognize that money is a survival line, particularly for those living in urban contexts where self-reliance is constrained. Living out human and planetary values under an extractive economic system often requires putting immediate financial security or quick gain on the line. This is real, difficult, and scary work.
Our governance does not ask contributors to martyr themselves or ignore economic survival. Instead, it seeks to build spaces, software, and trade mechanics where we can sustain our lives without resorting to exploitation, speculative hype, or selling out core values for venture capital metrics.
2. Relational Value Over Extractive Scale
Value is not limited to revenue or data points. Real value resides in ecological health, labor dignity, care, and community resilience.
 * Market trade is valid: We support localized, sensible trade where makers and growers are paid fairly for their craft.
 * Capitalist extraction is rejected: We draw a firm line against infinite growth mandates, opaque supply chains, and business models that rely on cheapened labor or unmeasured ecological harm.
3. Data Sovereignty & Contextual Boundaries
Digital tools are not neutral; they can easily become instruments of biopiracy and corporate harvesting.
 * Protection of Sensitive Knowledge: Indigenous, ancestral, and localized crop data must remain under the sovereignty of the communities that cultivate them.
 * Local-First Architecture: We prioritize local, offline, and community-controlled data models over broad, centralized open APIs that can be co-opted by industrial agriculture.
 * Nuance Over Generalization: We reject the software impulse to smooth over local context for the sake of hyper-abstract code.
4. Responsive Governance
This repository is a shared space, but it is not a blank canvas for speculative tech experiments. Decisions are guided by care, responsibility to the living world, and the realities of human-scale work. If a proposed feature or architectural change optimizes developer convenience by creating opacity, hostility, or extraction for real-world stewards, it will not be merged.
