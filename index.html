Relate | A Relational Planting Toolkit

Every second, the world loses roughly four football fields of healthy soil. By 2050, an estimated 95% of Earth's land could be degraded. The primary driver is decades of intensive monoculture farming—growing the same crop on the same land season after season—which strips the soil of organic matter and biological diversity.

This platform is a relational agronomic planner designed to counteract soil degradation. By combining location data, soil parameters (pH and type), and live environmental metrics, the system calculates deterministic companion planting strategies and daily field execution schedules to restore soil health while maintaining productive yields.

Key Capabilities

  • Site-Specific Agronomy: Maps soil pH, soil texture, and location parameters directly against target crop profiles.
  • Relational Companion Matching: Evaluates complementary crop pairings (e.g., nitrogen fixers alongside heavy feeders) to optimize yield and soil regeneration.
  • Automated Data Orchestration: Uses Apache Airflow and dbt to ingest live weather and soil APIs into precomputed feature tables for immediate client responses.
  • Actionable Field Scheduling: Converts complex seasonal windows into a daily planting planner for growers.
